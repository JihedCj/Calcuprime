Mathematical Concepts Explained

Percentages: A percentage is a way of expressing a number as a fraction of 100. It's used to compare quantities and show how much something has increased or decreased. For example, a 25% increase means the new value is 1.25 times the original.

Fractions: Fractions represent parts of a whole number. They're used when you need to work with portions or divisions. Understanding fractions is crucial for advanced mathematics and practical applications.

Algebra: Algebra uses variables and equations to represent relationships and solve for unknown values. It's a fundamental tool in mathematics used to solve practical problems.

Geometry: Geometry deals with shapes, sizes, and properties of figures. It's essential for architecture, engineering, design, and understanding physical space.

Statistics: Statistics involves collecting, analyzing, and interpreting data. It helps make informed decisions based on data patterns and trends.
