Cookie Policy

Last Updated: November 20, 2024

Introduction

Welcome to CalcuPrime's Cookie Policy. This policy explains what cookies are, how we use them on our website, and how you can manage your cookie preferences. By using CalcuPrime, you agree to the use of cookies in accordance with this policy.

We are committed to being transparent about how we use cookies and respecting your privacy. This policy complies with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and other applicable data protection laws.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device (computer, smartphone, or tablet) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and provide a better user experience.

Cookies allow websites to:

  • Remember your preferences and settings
  • Understand how you use the website
  • Improve website functionality and performance
  • Provide personalized content and advertisements
  • Analyze website traffic and user behavior

Cookies can be set by the website you're visiting (first-party cookies) or by third-party services that the website uses (third-party cookies).

Types of Cookies We Use

CalcuPrime uses different types of cookies to provide you with the best possible experience. Below is a detailed breakdown of the cookies we use:

Cookie Type Purpose Duration Required?
Essential Cookies These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly. They enable core functionality such as security, network management, and accessibility. Session / Persistent Yes
Analytics Cookies These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting and reporting information anonymously. Up to 2 years No
Advertising Cookies These cookies are used to deliver advertisements that are relevant to you. They also limit the number of times you see an ad and help measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns. Up to 2 years No
Functionality Cookies These cookies allow the website to remember choices you make (such as your preferred calculator settings) and provide enhanced, personalized features. Up to 1 year No
Performance Cookies These cookies collect information about how you use our website, such as which pages you visit most often, to help us improve website performance. Up to 2 years No

1. Essential Cookies

These cookies are strictly necessary for the operation of CalcuPrime. They include:

  • Session cookies: Keep you logged in during your visit
  • Security cookies: Protect against malicious activity and ensure secure access
  • Load balancing cookies: Distribute website traffic across servers
  • Cookie consent cookies: Remember your cookie preferences

Note: Essential cookies cannot be disabled as they are necessary for the website to function. Disabling them may prevent you from using certain features of CalcuPrime.

2. Analytics Cookies

We use analytics cookies to understand how visitors use our website. This helps us improve our services and user experience. These cookies collect information such as:

  • Number of visitors to our website
  • Which pages are visited most frequently
  • How long visitors spend on each page
  • Which calculators are used most often
  • Geographic location of visitors (country/city level)
  • Browser type and device information

All information collected by analytics cookies is aggregated and anonymous. We cannot identify individual users from this data.

3. Advertising Cookies

We use advertising cookies to display relevant advertisements to our visitors. These cookies:

  • Track your browsing activity across different websites
  • Build a profile of your interests to show relevant ads
  • Limit the number of times you see the same advertisement
  • Measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns
  • Help us generate revenue to keep CalcuPrime free for all users

These cookies are placed by third-party advertising networks with our permission. You can opt out of personalized advertising by managing your cookie preferences (see "How to Manage Cookies" section below).

Google AdSense Cookies

CalcuPrime uses Google AdSense to display advertisements on our website. Google AdSense uses cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to our website and other websites on the Internet.

How Google AdSense Uses Cookies

  • Personalized Advertising: Google uses cookies to show you ads based on your interests and previous browsing activity
  • Ad Frequency: Cookies help control how often you see specific ads
  • Performance Measurement: Track which ads are most effective
  • Fraud Prevention: Detect and prevent invalid clicks and ad impressions

Google AdSense Cookie Types

  • IDE: Used to measure ad performance and provide relevant ads (Duration: 1-2 years)
  • DSID: Links your activity across devices if you're signed in to Google (Duration: 2 weeks)
  • __gads: Registers user actions and targets content based on ad clicks (Duration: 1 year)
  • __gac: Contains campaign-related information for the user (Duration: 90 days)

Opting Out of Google Ads

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting:

Google Ad Settings Google Ads Preferences Digital Advertising Alliance European Interactive Digital Advertising Alliance

Important: Opting out of personalized advertising does not mean you will see fewer ads. It means the ads you see will not be personalized based on your interests. You will still see generic advertisements.

For more information about how Google uses cookies, please visit Google's Cookie Policy.

Google Analytics Cookies

CalcuPrime uses Google Analytics, a web analytics service provided by Google Inc., to understand how our website is used and to improve user experience. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect information about your use of our website.

Information Collected by Google Analytics

  • Pages you visit and the order in which you visit them
  • Time spent on each page
  • How you arrived at our website (search engine, direct link, referral, etc.)
  • Your approximate geographic location (country, region, city)
  • Browser type, operating system, and device information
  • Screen resolution and JavaScript support
  • Network provider information

Google Analytics Cookie Types

  • _ga: Distinguishes unique users (Duration: 2 years)
  • _gid: Distinguishes unique users (Duration: 24 hours)
  • _gat: Throttles request rate to limit data collection (Duration: 1 minute)
  • _gac_<property-id>: Contains campaign information (Duration: 90 days)
  • AMP_TOKEN: Contains a token used to retrieve a Client ID (Duration: 30 seconds to 1 year)

IP Anonymization

We have enabled IP anonymization on Google Analytics, which means your IP address is truncated within EU member states and other parties to the European Economic Area Agreement. Only in exceptional cases is the full IP address sent to Google servers in the USA and shortened there.

Data Usage by Google Analytics

The information generated by Google Analytics cookies about your use of CalcuPrime is transmitted to and stored by Google on servers in the United States. Google uses this information to:

  • Evaluate your use of our website
  • Compile reports on website activity for website operators
  • Provide other services relating to website activity and internet usage

Opting Out of Google Analytics

You can prevent Google Analytics from collecting your data by:

  • Disabling cookies in your browser settings (see "How to Manage Cookies" section below)
  • Installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on
  • Using browser privacy/incognito mode

For more information about Google Analytics and privacy, please visit Google Analytics Privacy Policy.

How to Manage and Disable Cookies

You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies. You can exercise your cookie preferences in several ways:

1. Browser Settings

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can set your browser to:

  • Block all cookies
  • Accept cookies only from specific websites
  • Delete all cookies when you close your browser
  • Warn you before a cookie is set

Browser-Specific Cookie Management Instructions

Click on your browser below for detailed instructions on managing cookies:

Google Chrome Mozilla Firefox Apple Safari Microsoft Edge Opera Brave Browser

2. Mobile Device Settings

For mobile devices, you can manage cookies through your device settings:

  • iOS: Settings → Safari → Block All Cookies
  • Android: Settings → Privacy → Clear browsing data

3. Third-Party Cookie Management Tools

You can also use third-party tools to manage cookies:

  • Digital Advertising Alliance (DAA) Opt-Out Tool
  • European Interactive Digital Advertising Alliance (EDAA)
  • Network Advertising Initiative (NAI) Opt-Out Tool

Warning: Disabling cookies may affect the functionality of CalcuPrime and other websites you visit. Some features may not work properly, and your user experience may be degraded. Essential cookies are necessary for the website to function and cannot be disabled.

4. "Do Not Track" Signals

Some browsers have a "Do Not Track" (DNT) feature that signals to websites that you do not want to be tracked. However, there is currently no industry standard for how to respond to DNT signals. CalcuPrime does not currently respond to DNT signals, but we respect your privacy choices made through browser cookie settings and opt-out tools.

  • Right to be Informed: You have the right to know what cookies are being used and why
  • Right to Choose: You have the right to accept or reject non-essential cookies
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You can change your cookie preferences at any time
  • Right to Access: You have the right to know what data has been collected via cookies
  • Right to Deletion: You have the right to request deletion of data collected via cookies

Withdrawing Cookie Consent
