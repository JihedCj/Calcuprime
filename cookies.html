1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device (computer, smartphone, or tablet) when you visit websites. They help websites remember your preferences and provide a better browsing experience.

2. How We Use Cookies

CalcuPrime uses cookies for the following purposes:

2.1 Essential Cookies (Always Active)

These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly:

  • Cookie Consent: Remembers your cookie preferences
  • Session Management: Maintains your session while using calculators
  • Security: Protects against fraudulent activity

2.2 Analytics Cookies (Requires Consent)

These cookies help us understand how visitors use our site:

  • Google Analytics: Tracks page views, time on site, bounce rate
  • Usage Patterns: Which calculators are most popular
  • Performance: Page load times and errors

2.3 Advertising Cookies (Requires Consent)

These cookies enable personalized advertising:

  • Google AdSense: Displays relevant advertisements
  • Ad Personalization: Shows ads based on your interests
  • Ad Performance: Measures ad effectiveness

4. Managing Cookies

4.1 Our Cookie Consent Tool

When you first visit CalcuPrime, you'll see our cookie consent banner. You can:

  • Accept All: Enable all cookies (essential, analytics, advertising)
  • Reject All: Only essential cookies will be used
  • Learn More: Read our Privacy Policy for detailed information

Your choice is stored for 1 year. You can change your preferences at any time by clearing your browser's local storage.

4.2 Browser Settings

You can also manage cookies through your browser settings:

  • Chrome: Settings > Privacy and security > Cookies and other site data
  • Firefox: Options > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences > Privacy > Manage Website Data
  • Edge: Settings > Privacy, search, and services > Cookies

4.3 Opt-Out Links

  • Google Analytics: Browser Add-on
  • Google Ads: Ad Settings
  • Network Advertising: NAI Opt-Out

5. Third-Party Cookies

CalcuPrime uses third-party services that may set cookies:

  • Google Analytics: Website analytics - Privacy Policy
  • Google AdSense: Advertising - How Google uses cookies

6. Do Not Track Signals

Some browsers have a "Do Not Track" (DNT) feature. Currently, CalcuPrime does not respond to DNT signals, but we respect your cookie preferences set through our cookie consent banner.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
