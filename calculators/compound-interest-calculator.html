Compound Interest Formula

A = P(1 + r/n)^(nt)

Where:
A = Final amount
P = Initial principal
r = Annual interest rate (decimal)
n = Compound frequency per year
t = Time in years

What is Compound Interest?

Compound interest is interest calculated on the initial principal and also on the accumulated interest from previous periods. It's often called "interest on interest" and makes your money grow faster than simple interest.

Compound Frequency

  • Daily: Interest compounds 365 times per year
  • Monthly: Interest compounds 12 times per year
  • Quarterly: Interest compounds 4 times per year
  • Semi-annually: Interest compounds 2 times per year
  • Annually: Interest compounds once per year
