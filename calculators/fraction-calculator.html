Fraction Operations

Fractions represent parts of a whole. Operations follow specific rules to maintain mathematical accuracy.

How to Calculate Fractions

Addition & Subtraction

Find common denominator, then add/subtract numerators: 1/4 + 1/2 = 1/4 + 2/4 = 3/4

Multiplication

Multiply numerators together and denominators together: 1/2 × 3/4 = 3/8

Division

Flip the second fraction and multiply: 1/2 ÷ 1/4 = 1/2 × 4/1 = 4/2 = 2
