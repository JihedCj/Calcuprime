How to Use the Loan Calculator

Our loan calculator helps you determine your monthly payment, total interest, and overall cost for any type of loan including personal loans, auto loans, student loans, and more.

  1. Loan Amount: Enter the total amount you need to borrow
  2. Interest Rate: Enter the annual interest rate (APR)
  3. Loan Term: Select how many months or years you'll take to repay
  4. Calculate: View your monthly payment and total loan cost

Loan Payment Formula

The monthly payment is calculated using the following formula:

M = P × [r(1+r)^n] / [(1+r)^n-1]

Where:
M = Monthly payment
P = Principal loan amount
r = Monthly interest rate (annual rate ÷ 12)
n = Number of payments (months)

Types of Loans

Personal Loans

Unsecured loans that can be used for any purpose. Typically range from $1,000 to $50,000 with terms of 2-7 years.

Auto Loans

Secured loans for vehicle purchases. Terms typically range from 3-7 years with the vehicle serving as collateral.

Student Loans

Education financing with terms of 10-25 years. Federal student loans often have lower rates and more flexible repayment options.

Home Equity Loans

Secured loans using your home's equity as collateral. Terms typically range from 5-30 years.

Tips for Getting the Best Loan

  • Check your credit score before applying - higher scores get better rates
  • Compare offers from multiple lenders
  • Consider the total cost, not just the monthly payment
  • Avoid extending the loan term just to lower payments
  • Make extra payments when possible to reduce interest
  • Read all terms and conditions carefully before signing

Frequently Asked Questions

What's a good interest rate for a personal loan?

Personal loan rates typically range from 6% to 36% depending on your credit score. Those with excellent credit (720+) can get rates as low as 6-10%.

Should I choose a shorter or longer loan term?

Shorter terms have higher monthly payments but lower total interest. Longer terms have lower monthly payments but cost more overall. Choose based on your budget and financial goals.

Can I pay off my loan early?

Many loans allow early repayment, but some have prepayment penalties. Check your loan agreement before making extra payments.
