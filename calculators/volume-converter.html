Common Volume Conversions

1 Liter = 1,000 milliliters = 33.814 fl oz = 4.227 cups

1 Gallon (US) = 3.785 liters = 128 fl oz = 16 cups = 4 quarts

1 Cup = 236.588 ml = 8 fl oz = 16 tablespoons

1 Tablespoon = 14.787 ml = 3 teaspoons

1 Quart = 0.946 liters = 32 fl oz = 4 cups

Practical Applications

Cooking & Baking

Recipe conversions between metric and imperial. European recipes use ml/L, American use cups/tbsp. Accurate volume conversion ensures recipe success.

Automotive

Engine displacement (cubic centimeters or liters), fuel capacity (gallons or liters), oil changes (quarts or liters). US cars: gallons, European: liters.

Aquariums

Tank sizes in gallons or liters. Water treatment doses based on volume. A 50-gallon tank = 189 liters.

Swimming Pools

Pool volume for chemicals. Olympic pool: 2.5M liters (660,000 gallons). Residential: 15,000-25,000 gallons.
