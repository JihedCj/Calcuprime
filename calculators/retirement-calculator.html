How to Use the Retirement Calculator

This calculator helps you estimate how much you'll have saved for retirement based on your current savings, regular contributions, and expected investment returns.

Retirement Planning Tips

  • Start saving as early as possible to maximize compound growth
  • Take full advantage of employer 401(k) matching
  • Aim to save 15% of your income for retirement
  • Diversify your retirement portfolio
  • Increase contributions when you get raises
  • Consider both traditional and Roth retirement accounts

How Much Do You Need?

Financial experts typically recommend having 10-12 times your final salary saved by retirement. The 4% rule suggests you can safely withdraw 4% of your retirement savings annually without running out of money.
