Understanding Discounts

Discounts reduce the original price by a percentage. A 20% discount on $100 saves you $20, making the final price $80.

Common Discount Scenarios

  • Seasonal Sales: 20-50% off during major holidays
  • Clearance: 50-70% off end-of-season items
  • Bundle Discounts: Buy more, save more promotions
  • Loyalty Programs: Member-exclusive discounts

Shopping Tips

  • Compare final prices after discount, not just percentages
  • Stack coupons when allowed for maximum savings
  • Consider quality - sometimes full price is better value
  • Watch for "fake" discounts with inflated original prices
