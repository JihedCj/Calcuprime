CalcuPrime is a professional online calculator platform providing free, accurate, and easy-to-use calculators and converters for finance, health, chemistry, and everyday calculations. Our mission is to make complex calculations simple and accessible to everyone, anywhere.

Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly will I receive a response?
We aim to respond to all inquiries within 24-48 hours during business days (Monday-Friday). Messages received during weekends or holidays will be addressed on the next business day.
Are CalcuPrime calculators really free to use?
Yes! All our calculators and converters are completely free to use. We support our service through ethical advertising, which helps us maintain and improve our platform without charging users.
How can I report a bug or error in a calculator?
Please use the contact form above and select "Bug Report" as the subject. Include details about which calculator you were using, what inputs you provided, and what the unexpected result was. Screenshots are helpful if possible.
Can I suggest a new calculator or feature?
Absolutely! We love hearing suggestions from our users. Select "Feedback & Suggestions" or "Feature Request" in the contact form and tell us what calculator or feature you'd like to see. We review all suggestions and prioritize based on user demand.
Do you offer API access or white-label solutions?
For business inquiries including API access, white-label solutions, or partnership opportunities, please email us at business@calcuprime.com with details about your requirements.
How do you ensure the accuracy of your calculators?
All our calculators are developed using industry-standard formulas and undergo thorough testing. We regularly update our calculators based on user feedback and changing standards. However, for critical decisions, we always recommend consulting with a qualified professional.
Is my data secure when I use your calculators?
Yes. All calculations are performed locally in your browser, and we do not store or transmit your calculation data to our servers. Read our Privacy Policy for complete details on how we handle data.
Can I use CalcuPrime calculators on my mobile device?
Yes! Our entire platform is fully responsive and optimized for mobile devices, tablets, and desktops. You can access all features from any device with a web browser.
